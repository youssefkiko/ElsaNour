# Decisions and assumptions

Every assumption made while building is recorded here, newest at the bottom of each phase.

## Phase 0: Foundation

1. **Three languages from the start (owner decision, 2026-11-22).** The owner chose Arabic, French and English. Arabic is the default and is RTL; French and English are LTR. This replaces the PRD line "French may be added later".
2. **Language in the URL.** Each page lives under `/ar`, `/fr` or `/en`. Visiting `/` redirects to `/ar`. No automatic detection from the browser language yet, so ad links always land in Arabic unless they point to `/fr` or `/en`.
3. **No i18n library.** Translations are plain JSON files loaded on the server, following the official Next.js guide. This keeps the site light; a library like `next-intl` can be added later if needed.
4. **Arabic register.** Website text uses simple Modern Standard Arabic. The owner can ask to switch any text to Darija.
5. **Fonts (temporary).** Cairo for all text (supports Arabic and Latin) and Cormorant Garamond for the "Elsanour" wordmark. The owner will confirm fonts after seeing the full design.
6. **Brand colors (temporary).** Emerald green `#06372a` / `#0b5d45` and gold `#c9a24b`, to be adjusted to the official logo once provided.
7. **No dark mode.** The brand look stays the same on every device.
8. **Project folder.** The app lives in `elsanour-store/` inside the owner's folder (the PRD PDF stays outside the app). The folder is inside OneDrive; if syncing `node_modules` becomes slow, the project can be moved outside OneDrive.
9. **Domain.** Phase 0 uses the free Vercel URL. A custom domain will be bought later (Phase 8).
10. **Git author.** Commits use a local placeholder author (`Elsanour`) until the owner's GitHub account exists.
11. **Instagram link.** `https://www.instagram.com/elsanour.ma/` (from the PRD).
12. **New logo, black and gold (owner decision, 2026-11-22).** The owner provided a new logo (black and gold) and asked to follow it. This replaces the PRD's emerald green: the site now uses black (`noir-*`, main `#070707`) and metallic gold (`gold-*`, main `#d4a64a`). Items 5 and 6 above are replaced by this entry and the next one.
13. **Logo mark.** The logo was provided as a JPG mockup on a fabric background, not a clean file. The illustration (woman and hands) was extracted to `public/brand/elsanour-mark.png` with a transparent background. It only looks right on dark backgrounds. A clean vector (SVG) or transparent PNG from the designer is still needed for light backgrounds, printing and sharp small sizes.
14. **Wordmark font.** The owner did not like the font of the name in the logo and let us choose. "Elsanour" is now written in code with **Bodoni Moda** (high-contrast fashion serif), uppercase with wide letter spacing and a metallic gold gradient. Body text stays Cairo for now.
15. **Ring O (final).** The owner chose the first ring drawing (two overlapping crescents: heavy left and right sides, hairlines at top and bottom, line-art diamond with a sparkle).
   As in the logo, the O of the wordmark is a gold ring with a diamond on top, drawn as an inline SVG in `src/components/Wordmark.tsx` so it stays sharp at every size.

## Phase 1: Layout and homepage

1. **Header and footer in the layout.** They appear on every page. The header is sticky; on mobile it shows the wordmark and a menu button, on desktop the links and the language switcher.
2. **Menu links point to sections of the home page** (`#packs`, `#why`, `#contact`). In Phase 2 the "Packs" link will point to the new packs page.
3. **Language switcher goes to the home page** of the other language. Keeping the same page when switching will be added in Phase 2, when there are more pages.
4. **Customer is addressed in the feminine** in Arabic (اكتشفي، تابعينا), since the customers are women.
5. **Placeholder packs.** Four example packs with names, contents and prices (249, 279 instead of 329, 349, 229 MAD) are placeholders. The cards show "ordering opens soon" and are not clickable yet. Real data comes in Phase 2.
6. **Prices** are shown as "249 درهم" (Arabic), "249 DH" (French), "249 MAD" (English).
7. **No phone number in the footer yet.** The store phone will come from the settings (Phase 5). For now the footer shows Instagram only.
8. **Smooth scrolling** uses Next.js 16's `data-scroll-behavior="smooth"` and `scroll-padding-top` so sections are not hidden under the sticky header.
9. **Images in AVIF/WebP** (`next.config.ts`) to keep the hero image small on mobile.
10. **Copyright year** is written as 2026 in the footer (a fixed value, to keep the page fully static).
11. **3D ring hero (owner decision, 2026-11-22).** Inspired by the scroll effect of a luxury jewelry landing page the owner showed us. We tried turning the logo woman into 3D (TRELLIS.2): the owner did not like the shape. Plan B, chosen by the owner: a gold ring with a diamond, built in code with **three.js** (no model file, no license to credit). It turns with the scroll; on phones it rises and the headline appears below, on wide screens it slides to the side and the headline appears on the other side. Our own code, not copied from the reference site.
12. **three.js loads late.** It loads on the first touch, scroll or mouse move (or after 10 seconds). Until then a static SVG ring is shown. This keeps Lighthouse mobile at about 87–97 locally (it dropped to 70 when three.js loaded during the test). It pauses when the hero is off screen, and the ring does not spin for people who ask their phone for reduced motion.
13. **Pack cards fly in from the sides in 3D** with pure CSS scroll-driven animations (no library). Browsers without support (e.g. older Firefox) just show the cards normally.
14. **The logo woman illustration is no longer in the hero.** The file stays in `public/brand/` for later use.
15. **REA (morluto/rea) not installed.** It is a reverse-engineering tool, not needed for the design. It can be added later if the owner wants.
16. **3D ring replaced by the logo woman (owner decision, 2026-11-22).** The owner did not like the 3D ring. The hero now shows the logo woman illustration with the same scroll behavior: it turns in 3D perspective (CSS `rotateY`), rises and shrinks on phones or slides to the side on wide screens, then the headline appears. Pure CSS driven by the scroll progress, so **three.js was removed** (items 11 and 12 above no longer apply).
17. **3 packs per row (owner request).** From 640px wide: 3 columns (side cards fly in from their side, the middle one from the depth). Phones under 640px keep 2 columns so the cards stay readable. Two more example packs were added (6 in total: 249, 279/329, 349, 229, 299, 259/309 MAD, all placeholders) so the rows are full.
18. **Categories instead of packs on the home page (owner decision, 2026-11-22).** The store now sells 5 categories: necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets and gift packs. This extends the PRD (which only had packs). The home page shows 5 photo tiles with a white label (like a fashion store); clicking a tile opens `/[lang]/category/[slug]`. In Phase 1 that page only shows the category name and "coming soon"; the product list comes in Phase 2. The data model in Phase 2 will need a `category` field on products.
19. **Tile layout.** Desktop: 5 in a row. Tablet: 3 then 2. Phone: 2 per row, the packs tile takes the full last row. Each tile flies in from its side in 3D when scrolling. Tiles show a gold line icon until the owner sends one photo per category.
20. **Menu and hero button** now point to the collections ("المجموعات" / "تسوقي الآن"). Items 2 and 5 of Phase 1 are replaced by this.
21. **Branded 404 page** (`src/app/[lang]/not-found.tsx`), in the 3 languages, inside the site layout. Added now because the default black 404 looked broken. Category pages use `export const instant = false` so an unknown slug can show this 404 without the dev warning (`dynamicParams` is not allowed with `cacheComponents`).
22. **Category tiles: 3 per row (owner request).** From 640px wide: first row necklaces, earrings, rings; second row bracelets and packs, same size, centered. No more 5 in one row on desktop (replaces the layout in item 19). Phones keep 2 per row with packs on the full last row.
23. **Watches category added (owner request).** 6 categories now: necklaces, earrings, rings, bracelets, watches, packs. All tiles the same size: 3 per row on tablet and desktop (2 full rows), 2 per row on phones (3 rows). Replaces the centered second row of item 22.

Otherwise the logo woman is shown (current live site). The video is re-encoded with a keyframe every 2 frames so seeking is smooth (about 1 MB for 10 s).

## Phase 2: Product catalog (static data)

1. **Catalog by category.** The PRD planned 4 placeholder packs; since the owner moved to 6 categories, `src/data/products.ts` has 3 placeholder products per category (18). Names, prices and contents are placeholders until the owner sends the real ones. One product (Classic Gold Watch) is marked out of stock to show that state.
2. **Routes.** Category page `/[lang]/category/[slug]` lists its products (2 per row on phones, 3 from 640px). Product page `/[lang]/product/[slug]`. Unknown slugs show the branded 404.
3. **Product page.** Swipeable gallery (native scroll-snap, works in RTL, dots to jump), name, price in MAD with the old price crossed and "save X%", what is in the box, delivery and cash-on-delivery note, "You may also like" (same category).
4. **Order button.** Until Phase 3 it shows "online ordering opens soon". On phones it is repeated in a bar fixed at the bottom with the price. Out-of-stock products have a disabled button.
5. **Photos.** Products have an `images` list (files in `/public/products`). While empty, 3 clean placeholder slides with the category icon are shown.
6. **Descriptions.** One short description per category for now (dictionaries `catalog.blurb`); per-product descriptions can come with the real data (Phase 5).
7. **Language switcher keeps the page** (e.g. /ar/product/x → /fr/product/x).
8. **Collections background photo (owner request, 2026-11-23).** The owner's image "BB" (emerald sculpted woman with gold and pearl jewelry, 736x736) is the background of the home collections section (`public/brand/collections-bg.jpg`), darkened with a gradient so titles stay readable. The section is always dark (`data-theme="dark"`) in both themes. The tiles became semi-transparent "glass" (light 2px backdrop blur) so the photo shows through them. Source of the image to be confirmed by the owner (736px squares are typical of Pinterest downloads).
9. **Theme script without React warning.** The morning/evening script is now inserted with `useServerInsertedHTML` (`ThemeScript`), as recommended by the Next.js guide "Preventing flash before hydration", instead of a `<script>` in the layout (which triggered the dev "1 Issue").
10. **3D emerald gem buttons (owner choice "3D 3", 2026-11-23).** All buttons and labels became cut emerald gems with depth (`.gem` + `.gem-face` in globals.css): main buttons (shop now, order now, 404 back), category labels on the tiles, other-category links (dark gems with gold text), discount and selected language (small chips), and out of stock (a sunken gem that cannot be pressed). They press down when clicked, a light sweeps across on hover, and on computers they tilt towards the mouse (`GemTilt`, one listener for the page; not on touch screens or with reduced motion). Pure CSS shapes, no images. Fixed emerald/gold colors in both themes.

## Phase 3: Basket, checkout and database

1. **Golden basket instead of one product per order (owner decision, 2026-11-23).** The PRD said "one pack per order, no multi-product cart". The owner asked for a basket where the customer collects several products and orders them together, with one delivery. The product page and every product card now have an "add to basket" gem (the direct "order now" button was removed, owner's choice). Limits: 1 to 5 pieces per product, 10 different products at most.
2. **Basket look and motion.** A woven golden basket (SVG drawn in the code, no image) in an emerald round button at the bottom of the screen (bottom-left on the Arabic site). It only appears once something is in it, pops out with a small bounce, and the product photo flies into it in an arc while the basket wobbles and its counter bumps. With "reduce motion" there is no flight, only the counter. On product pages on phones it floats above the buy bar. React Three Fiber was considered (owner question) and not used: it is not installed, and a 3D library would make phones on 4G slower for an effect that CSS/SVG already does.
3. **Where the basket lives.** In the visitor's browser (localStorage), so it survives a refresh and is shared between tabs. Only product slugs and quantities are stored; names and prices always come from the product data, and products that disappear or go out of stock are removed automatically. The basket is emptied when the thank-you page opens.
4. **Basket panel.** Tapping the basket opens a panel (slides up from the bottom on phones, from the side on computers): products with photo, category, name, unit price, quantity box (− / +), remove link, free-delivery progress, total, "checkout", "keep shopping" and three reassurances (cash on delivery, delivery everywhere, gift box).
5. **Design of the basket and checkout (owner request, 2026-11-23).** Inspired by high-end jewelry shops: hairline borders, generous spacing, fields with an icon and a label that floats up when the field is used, numbered sections ("1 Your details", "2 Delivery address"), errors written under the field with an icon. **Square corners** on every field, box and panel (owner request); only the floating basket button and its counter stay round. On phones the order summary folds into one bar that shows the total.
6. **Free delivery from 300 MAD (owner decision, 2026-11-23).** When the basket total (products only) is 300 MAD or more, delivery is free. Below that: 20 MAD for Casablanca, 35 MAD for other cities (placeholders until the owner gives the real fees). The threshold and the fees are in the `settings` table (editable from the admin in Phase 5). The basket and the checkout show "only X MAD more for free delivery" with a gold line, and the product page says "free delivery from 300 MAD". Assumption: "above 300" is read as "300 or more"; easy to change.
7. **Database.** Supabase project in West EU (Ireland), Free plan. `supabase/migrations/0001_orders_settings.sql` (pasted once by the owner in Supabase > SQL Editor) creates `settings`, `orders` and `order_items` (one row per product of the order), plus a `place_order` function that saves the order and its products together (all or nothing). Row Level Security is ON with no public policy: the browser (publishable key) can neither read nor write these tables, and only the server (secret key, `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY`, only in `.env.local` and Vercel) may call `place_order`. Admin policies come in Phase 4.
8. **Product reference in orders.** Products are still static data in the code, so each order line stores `product_slug` plus the product name and unit price copied at order time (as the PRD asks). The `product_id` link comes with the products table in Phase 5. The saved product name is the Arabic one, whatever the site language.
9. **Order number.** `reference` is a counter starting at 1001 (shown as #1001), short and easy to say on the phone.
10. **Phone.** Moroccan mobiles only: 06/07, +212 6/7 or 00212 6/7; spaces, dots and dashes are accepted. Saved as `+2126XXXXXXXX` (ready for tap-to-call and wa.me in Phase 4).
11. **City.** Searchable list of about 100 Moroccan cities (Arabic and Latin names, accents ignored). A small town that is not in the list can still be typed, so no customer is blocked. A known city is saved with its Latin name (e.g. "Casablanca"). Only the city of Casablanca itself gets the Casablanca fee (not Bouskoura, Dar Bouazza, Mohammedia...).
12. **Totals on the server.** The browser shows a live summary, but the server recomputes everything (prices from the product data, delivery from the settings); nothing sent by the browser is trusted for prices. If the settings cannot be read, the placeholder values above are used.
13. **Validation.** One Zod schema (`src/lib/order.ts`) is used in the browser (instant messages, focus on the first wrong field, typed values kept) and again on the server. Messages are in Arabic on the Arabic site, and in French/English on the other languages.
14. **Protection.** Double submit: the button is locked while sending. Bots: a hidden honeypot field (a bot that fills it sees a thank-you page, nothing is saved). Duplicates: the same phone with the same basket within 10 minutes is not saved again; the customer sees the existing order number with a note. Rate limit: at most 8 orders per connection per 10 minutes (generous because Moroccan mobile networks share IP addresses); only a SHA-256 hash of the IP is stored, never the IP.
15. **UTM.** When a visitor lands from an ad link with `utm_source` / `utm_campaign`, the proxy keeps them in a cookie (`els_utm`, 30 days, latest ad wins, HttpOnly), and the order saves them.
16. **Pages.** Checkout `/[lang]/checkout` and thank-you `/[lang]/checkout/thank-you?ref=1001` (order number + "we will call you to confirm"). Both are hidden from Google (noindex). Out-of-stock products cannot be added (sunken gem) and are refused by the server.
17. **Status values** are `new, confirmed, shipped, delivered, returned, cancelled` (Postgres enum), default `new`.
18. **"Discover more pieces" instead of "other collections" (owner request, 2026-11-23).** At the bottom of each category page, the row of category buttons became product photos from the other collections (up to 8, taking turns between collections, in-stock first), each with its "add to basket" button. Phones swipe them sideways; computers show 4 per row.
